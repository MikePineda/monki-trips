# Kiko

Original artwork for monki-trips: a travelling monkey with a backpack bigger
than he is and a passport that never quite fits in the pocket. Drawn as SVG
for this project (`docs/design/mascots/kiko/concept.svg` is the concept
sheet; `sheet.svg` here is the animation sheet, 64 × 64 frames, one row per
state). No third-party artwork, fonts or clip art were used.

Licence: AGPL-3.0-only, like the rest of the repository. Kiko's art colours
are his own and are not UI tokens.
